Privacy Policy

This policy explains the categories of information ParentGuard may access, collect, use, transmit or store through its parent application, the ChildGuard companion app, and related services.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Publication note: Replace any placeholders in this policy with your final legal entity details, data-retention periods, service-provider list and actual deletion endpoint before publishing. The policy must match the production app and backend.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the ParentGuard family-safety ecosystem, including the ParentGuard parent application, the ChildGuard supervised-device application, the Installer Wizard and this website.

2. Information we may process

  • Account information: authentication identifiers and information needed to create and maintain a family account.
  • Family and pairing information: parent identifier, child identifier, family identifier and pairing information used to connect authorized devices.
  • Location information: location coordinates and related location metadata when the supervised device has granted location access and location sharing is enabled.
  • Device/service information: app/service state, permission state and technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot and secure the service.
  • Live media: microphone audio, camera video and screen content when the parent initiates a supported live feature and the supervised device running ChildGuard has granted the required Android authorization. These are not described as permanently stored recordings unless the backend actually stores them.
  • Support information: information you voluntarily provide when contacting support.

3. Why we use information

  • Authenticate users and maintain family accounts.
  • Pair parent and supervised devices.
  • Provide requested parental-control and family-safety features.
  • Transmit live location or live media when the corresponding feature is enabled.
  • Maintain service reliability, security and troubleshooting.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Sensitive permissions

ChildGuard may request Android permissions on the child's device including location, camera, microphone, notifications, Usage Access and Accessibility, plus system authorization for screen capture. Each permission has a specific documented purpose within ParentGuard. See Data & Permissions.

5. Third-party services

The current application architecture may use Firebase services for authentication, cloud data and real-time data, and real-time/WebRTC infrastructure for live features. Replace this section with the exact production vendor list, links and processing roles before publication.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers that process data on our behalf, when required by law, or where necessary to protect users, the service or public safety. Live family data is intended for the authorized family relationship configured by the account.

7. Data retention

Retain information only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, legal obligations, security and legitimate operational needs. Before publication, specify concrete retention periods for account data, location history, logs and support records based on your actual backend behavior.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

ParentGuard is a parental-control product. Parents or guardians are responsible for using it lawfully and for providing any notices, permissions or consents required in their jurisdiction. Do not use the service to collect information about a child without the required parental/guardian authority.

10. Account deletion

If your service offers account creation, users must have an accessible deletion process. See Account Deletion. Your production implementation must delete associated account data when a valid deletion request is completed, subject to narrowly defined legal exceptions.

11. Your choices

You can control many Android permissions through device settings and can stop using features that require optional permissions. Revoking a permission may disable the related feature.

12. Contact

Email: parentguard.support@gmail.com

13. Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are published.
