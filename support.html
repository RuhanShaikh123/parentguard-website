Quick answers

Common troubleshooting checks.

Why does Android show a foreground-service notification?

Foreground services are used for ongoing functions such as location, microphone or screen sharing. Android uses notifications to make those ongoing services visible.

Why can't I install the ChildGuard APK?

Android may block installation from an unapproved source. Follow the official Wizard instructions and verify that the package is the official release.

Why did a permission disappear?

Android and device manufacturers can revoke, restrict or change permissions. Recheck the relevant system setting and the ChildGuard permission status screen.
