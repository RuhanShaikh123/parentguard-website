Quick answers

Common troubleshooting checks.

Why does Android show a foreground-service notification?

Foreground services are used for ongoing functions such as location, microphone or screen sharing. Android uses notifications to make those ongoing services visible to the device user.

Why can't I install the ChildGuard APK?

Android may block installation from an unapproved source. Follow the official Wizard instructions and verify that the package is the official release from the authorized ChildGuard download URL.

Why did a permission disappear?

Android and device manufacturers can revoke, restrict or change permissions. Recheck the relevant system setting and the ChildGuard permission status screen.

The device isn't pairing correctly. What should I check?

Ensure both devices have a network connection, the parent account is signed in, and you are using the pairing flow from within ParentGuard. Contact support if the problem persists.
