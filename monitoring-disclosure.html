Monitoring & Supervision Disclosure

ParentGuard is a parent/guardian-managed family safety platform. This page explains the nature of supervised-device functionality provided through ChildGuard in clear, transparent language.

ParentGuard is not a secret surveillance product. It is intended for lawful parental or guardian supervision of a device where the parent has the authority and responsibility to configure and supervise that device with ChildGuard.

What the supervised device may do

Depending on the configuration selected in ParentGuard and permissions granted on Android, the ChildGuard supervised-device application can provide location information and live safety capabilities such as audio, camera and screen sharing to the authorized parent experience.

⌖

Location

The supervised device can send location updates to ParentGuard when location sharing is enabled and the required Android permission is granted.

🎤

Microphone

Live audio can be provided when microphone access and the feature are enabled by the authorized parent.

📷

Camera

Live camera can be provided when camera access and the feature are enabled by the authorized parent.

▣

Screen

Screen sharing requires Android's system authorization and uses MediaProjection — it is not silent or invisible.

💻

Device controls

Accessibility and Usage Access may support disclosed parental-control functionality where implemented.

🔔

Notifications

Ongoing foreground services display Android notifications so the device user can see active service activity.

Responsible use

  • Only supervise devices you are authorized to supervise as a parent or guardian.
  • Do not use ParentGuard or ChildGuard to secretly monitor adults.
  • Do not attempt to conceal the app or bypass Android security protections.
  • Follow applicable laws and any required notices or consents.

Why this disclosure matters

Powerful device capabilities require honest explanations. We publish this disclosure so families, reviewers and users can understand the product before installing it. See also our Data & Permissions page and Privacy Policy.
