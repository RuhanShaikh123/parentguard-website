Monitoring & Supervision Disclosure

ChildGuard is a parent/guardian-managed family safety product. This page explains the nature of supervised-device functionality in clear language.

ChildGuard is not a secret surveillance product. It is intended for lawful parental or guardian supervision of a device where the user has the appropriate authority to configure and supervise that device.

What the supervised device may do

Depending on configuration and permissions, the supervised device can provide location information and live safety capabilities such as audio, camera and screen sharing to the authorized parent experience.

⌖

Location

The device can send location updates when location sharing is enabled and the required Android permission is granted.

🎤

Microphone

Live audio can be provided when microphone access and the feature are enabled by the authorized parent.

📷

Camera

Live camera can be provided when camera access and the feature are enabled by the authorized parent.

▣

Screen

Screen sharing requires Android's system authorization and uses MediaProjection — it is not silent or invisible.

💻

Device controls

Accessibility and Usage Access may support disclosed parental-control functionality where implemented.

🔔

Notifications

Ongoing foreground services display Android notifications so the device user can see service activity.

Responsible use

  • Only supervise devices you are authorized to supervise.
  • Do not use ChildGuard to secretly monitor adults.
  • Do not attempt to conceal the app or bypass Android security protections.
  • Follow applicable laws and any required notices or consents.

Why this disclosure matters

Powerful device capabilities require honest explanations. We publish this disclosure so families, reviewers and users can understand the product before installing it. See also our Data & Permissions page and Privacy Policy.
