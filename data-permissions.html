What is not promised

ChildGuard does not promise unlimited hidden access to a device. Android and Google Play impose technical and policy restrictions. Some capabilities require foreground services, visible system notifications, user authorization or special settings.

Live media and storage

Live audio, camera and screen functionality should not be described as permanent recording unless the production backend actually stores the media. If storage is added later, update this page, the privacy policy, Data Safety declarations and in-app disclosures before release.
