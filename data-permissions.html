Data & Permissions

A plain-language map of the sensitive Android capabilities requested by the ChildGuard supervised-device application within the ParentGuard platform and why they may be needed.

Permission table

Capability Why it may be requested Data/action involved Important conditions
Fine / coarse location Family location sharing Coordinates and location metadata Requires Android location permission; background operation may require additional Android/Play requirements.
Background location Continue location service when the app is in the background, where enabled by the parent Location updates Must be core to disclosed functionality and meet system/policy requirements.
Microphone Live audio check Microphone audio sent to the authorized parent live connection Requires microphone permission and foreground-service notification.
Camera Live camera check Camera video sent to the authorized parent live connection Requires camera permission and foreground-service notification.
MediaProjection Live screen view Screen content transmitted to the authorized parent connection Requires Android's system screen-capture authorization.
Accessibility Service Parental device-management actions where implemented Accessibility events/actions depending on the declared feature Subject to Android Accessibility policies; must not be used to bypass privacy/security controls.
Usage Access Device activity controls and app insights App usage/activity information User must grant Android Usage Access in system settings.
Notifications Service transparency and user-visible status Foreground-service notifications Android notification behavior may vary by OS/device.
Internet Cloud and real-time communication Network traffic for authentication, data and live features Requires network connectivity.
Production requirement: This table must be reviewed whenever a permission, SDK, backend service or data flow changes. The Play Console Data Safety form and in-app disclosures must match the actual release.

What is not promised

ParentGuard and ChildGuard do not promise unlimited hidden access to a device. Android and Google Play impose technical and policy restrictions. Some capabilities require foreground services, visible system notifications, user authorization or special settings.

Live media and storage

Live audio, camera and screen functionality are not described as permanent recordings unless the production backend actually stores the media. If storage is added later, update this page, the privacy policy, Data Safety declarations and in-app disclosures before release.
