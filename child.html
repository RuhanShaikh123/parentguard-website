ChildGuard

The supervised-device companion that runs the family-safety services selected during setup. Powerful Android permissions are explained before they are used.

Get setup Wizard →View permissions
Important: ChildGuard is not designed or marketed as a secret surveillance tool. Use it only for lawful parental/guardian supervision and family-safety purposes.
Capabilities

What the supervised device can provide.

Location service

A foreground location service can request location updates and send the current location to the paired family data path when permission is available.

Microphone service

A microphone foreground service can provide live audio to the authorized parent connection when microphone access is granted.

Camera service

A camera foreground service can support live camera functionality when the camera permission is granted.

Screen capture

Screen sharing uses Android MediaProjection and requires the operating system's screen-capture authorization.

Accessibility

The Accessibility Service may support parental control/device-management actions. Its use must remain within Android and Google Play policy requirements.

Usage Access

Usage Access may be used for device activity information and control features where implemented and enabled.
