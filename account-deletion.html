Account Deletion

Request deletion of your ParentGuard family account and associated data through the process described below.

Deletion process

How to delete your account.

1

Request deletion

Use the in-app account deletion option in ParentGuard when available, or contact support from the registered account email address.

2

Verify ownership

Support will verify that the deletion request belongs to the authorized account holder.

3

Disconnect devices

Paired family relationships and associated child device data will be unlinked as part of the deletion workflow.

4

Deletion

Production systems delete associated account data, subject to clearly stated legal exceptions.

Developer action before publishing: Replace this request flow with your actual automated deletion endpoint and exact deletion timelines. Do not claim that deletion is automated unless your backend implements it.
✉

Request by email

Send an email from your account email address to parentguard.support@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.
