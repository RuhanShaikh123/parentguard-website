Account Deletion

Request deletion of your ChildGuard account and associated account data through the process described below.

1

Request deletion

Use the in-app account deletion option when available, or contact support from the account email.

2

Verify ownership

Support may need enough information to confirm that the request belongs to the account holder.

3

Disconnect devices

Paired family relationships and associated data should be handled as part of the deletion workflow.

4

Deletion

Production systems should delete associated account data, subject to clearly stated legal exceptions.

Developer action before publishing: Replace this generic request flow with your actual automated deletion endpoint and exact deletion timelines. Do not claim that deletion is automated unless your backend implements it.

Request by email

Send an email from the account email address to parentguard.support@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.
