Your data and privacy

Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your ParentGuard account and associated personal data, including linked ChildGuard accounts and family connections. Follow the instructions below to submit a request.

Deletion request

How to request account deletion

Account deletion requests are currently handled manually by the ParentGuard support team.

1

Submit your request

Send an email from the email address registered with your ParentGuard account to our support team. Use the subject "Account Deletion Request".

2

Verify account ownership

Our support team will verify that you are authorized to request deletion. We may ask for additional account information to confirm ownership. Never send your password or authentication codes.

3

Review linked accounts

We will identify the ParentGuard account, linked ChildGuard account or accounts, family connections and associated device data covered by your request.

4

Process deletion

After verification, we will process the deletion of eligible account and associated personal data from our active production systems within 30 days, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

✉

Submit an account deletion request

Email our support team using the registered account email address. Include the account email address and identify whether you are requesting deletion of a ParentGuard account, a ChildGuard account, or the entire linked family account.

parentguard.support@gmail.com

Do not include passwords, one-time passwords, authentication codes or other login credentials in your email.

Data handling

What data will be deleted?

Following successful verification, we will delete or remove the following data associated with the requested account, where applicable:

Account information

Account profile information, registered email address, authentication account and associated account identifiers.

Family connections

Family ID associations, parent-child account links and paired-device relationships.

Location information

Location information associated with the linked account and child device.

Monitoring information

Associated live audio, camera, screen-mirroring and installed-application information stored by our services.

Device-related information

Associated device records, monitoring settings and account-linked application data.

Other associated personal data

Other personal information associated with the account that is not required to be retained under applicable law.

Retention and exceptions

Deletion timeline and retained data

Active production systems

We aim to complete verified account deletion requests within 30 days after confirming account ownership and receiving the information needed to process the request.

Backup retention

Data remaining in protected backups may persist for up to 90 days after deletion from active production systems. Such backup data is not restored or used for ordinary account operations and is removed through the normal backup-retention cycle.

Legal and security exceptions

Certain information may be retained for longer when required by applicable law, legitimate financial recordkeeping obligations, fraud prevention, security investigations or the establishment or defence of legal claims.

Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary for the relevant purpose and will be deleted when the retention requirement no longer applies.

Before requesting deletion

Important information

  • Deleting your account is a permanent action and may not be reversible.
  • Linked ChildGuard accounts and family connections may stop working when the associated family account is deleted.
  • You may lose access to account-related settings and monitoring information.
  • Uninstalling ParentGuard or ChildGuard alone does not constitute an account deletion request.
  • If you have an active subscription, cancel it separately through Google Play subscription settings. Deleting an account does not automatically guarantee cancellation of a Google Play subscription.
